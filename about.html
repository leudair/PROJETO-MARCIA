About Amani Miami

Miami Luxury, Personally Delivered

Not a marketplace. Not a booking engine. A team that treats every request as a relationship, coordinated by people who know South Florida from the inside.

Amani Miami was created for clients who expect more than transportation. Every interaction is designed to be polished, personal and effortless — from the first inquiry to final delivery.

The idea was simple: South Florida already has exotic car rental companies, yacht brokers and property managers. What it did not have was one team willing to be responsible for how all three come together for a single client, on a single occasion, without the client having to manage the coordination themselves.

What began as a small, privately owned exotic car collection grew — deliberately, not by accident — into a complete lifestyle brand. Every expansion (yacht charters, then premium residences, then a dedicated concierge function) happened because clients kept asking Amani Miami to handle "the rest of the trip," not just the car.

The Amani Standard

How We Decide What Carries Our Name

Nothing in the Amani Miami network is listed because it is available. It is listed because it passed a standard.

Privately Owned

The fleet is owned and maintained by Amani Miami, not aggregated from third-party listings. Condition and presentation are our responsibility, not a disclaimer.

Personally Inspected

Every vehicle, yacht and residence added to the network is reviewed in person before it is offered to a single client.

Continually Re-Evaluated

Presentation, performance and hospitality standards are re-checked on an ongoing basis — access is earned once, and kept only by staying at the standard.

Our Philosophy

Four Ideas That Shape Every Decision

01

One Relationship, Every Detail

Clients are assigned a single point of contact, not a rotating call center. That person coordinates the vehicle, the yacht, the residence and the itinerary around them, so a request is never explained twice and nothing is ever handed off without context.

02

Access, Not Inventory

We do not describe our fleet, yachts or residences as "available units." They are relationships — with owners, captains and property partners — that Amani Miami has personally vetted and continues to manage on the client's behalf.

03

Discretion as Standard

Some clients want to be seen arriving. Others do not want to be noticed at all. Amani Miami treats both preferences as equally important, and never assumes which one applies without asking.

04

Miami, Genuinely Understood

Recommendations come from people who actually know the difference between a Brickell Wednesday and a South Beach Saturday — not a script read from a script sheet. That local fluency is the reason clients ask us to plan, not just deliver.

Our Team

People Who Notice What a Checklist Cannot

The Amani Miami team is built around specialists, not order-takers — people who ask a second question before recommending a vehicle, who know when a client wants conversation and when they want silence, and who take responsibility for a reservation from the first message to the final drop-off.

We keep the team intentionally close to the work: the same people who inspect the fleet are often the ones confirming a client's delivery window. That proximity is what keeps the standard consistent as the brand grows.
